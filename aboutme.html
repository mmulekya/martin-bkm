WHO AM I?

My name is Namutyaba Suzan, born on 20th April 2003. I am a Ugandan by nationality, and my parents reside in Kasawo, Mukono District. I am a young, motivated, and determined individual with a strong desire for personal growth and a better future. I value family, education, and community, and I strive to use my skills and abilities to make a positive contribution wherever I am. I am passionate about learning new things and working toward my goals with commitment and responsibility.

MY HOBBY

My hobbies include listening to music and hard work. Music helps me relax, stay motivated, and express my emotions. It inspires me during both happy and challenging moments and gives me energy to keep moving forward. Hard work is also an important part of my life; I enjoy staying productive, learning new skills, and putting effort into everything I do. Through hard work, I aim to improve myself and achieve my goals while making a positive contribution to those around me.

MY RELATIONSHIP

In 2022, my life took a meaningful turn when I met Mulekya Martin. Born on 1st January 1997, Martin comes from the Mukonjo tribe and belongs to the Enjovu clan. From the time we met, our connection grew steadily, shaped by understanding, patience, and shared values. Our relationship has been a journey of learning, supporting one another, and facing life together with hope and commitment. Through the years, we have built a bond rooted in respect, trust, and a shared vision for the future, making our relationship an important and cherished part of my life.
